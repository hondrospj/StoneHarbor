Datum quick definitions
MLLW: Mean Lower Low Water — the average of the lower of the two daily low tides.
MSL: Mean Sea Level — the long-term average sea level over a standard period.
NAVD88: North American Vertical Datum of 1988 — a geodetic land-based elevation reference used on maps and surveys.
MHHW: Mean Higher High Water — the average of the higher of the two daily high tides.
